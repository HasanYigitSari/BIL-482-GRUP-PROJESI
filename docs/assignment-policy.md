# Assignment Policy: Group Contributions, Academic Integrity, and Final Evaluation

**Author:** Çiğdem Avcı

Dear Students,

I would like to clarify my expectations regarding group assignments, individual contributions, and academic integrity.

## 1. Group Work and Individual Contributions

All assignments must be completed exclusively by the students officially registered in the respective group.

Each group member is expected to make identifiable and verifiable contributions to the assignment.

In particular:

- Each student must use their own account and name when making commits, uploading files, or submitting contributions.
- Git commits and contribution histories must clearly reflect each student's actual work.
- Each group must explicitly document which student contributed to which files, components, features, or modifications.
- Contributions must be traceable to individual students through commit histories, file histories, and contribution records.

Statements such as "Only one person committed the code, but everyone contributed" will not be accepted as sufficient evidence of individual contributions.

Please organize your work and maintain proper contribution records from the beginning of the assignment.

## 2. Academic Integrity

You may use appropriate development tools, online resources, and AI tools, provided that the submitted work remains the group's own work.

The following practices are strictly prohibited:

- Outsourcing any part of the assignment to someone outside the group.
- Receiving unauthorized implementation assistance from individuals outside the group.
- Copying code, solutions, or assignment deliverables from other groups.
- Submitting work produced by someone outside the group as your own.

Any group found to have engaged in plagiarism, outsourcing, or other forms of academic dishonesty will receive a grade of zero (0) for the assignment. No exceptions will be made in confirmed cases.

## 3. Shared Responsibility and Project Understanding

Group assignments are intended to be collaborative learning activities, not simply a collection of independently completed tasks.

All group members are expected to:

- Work together throughout the assignment.
- Understand the overall architecture, implementation, and functionality of the submitted application.
- Be familiar with the codebase, including components developed primarily by other group members.
- Be able to explain and justify the group's design and implementation decisions.

Dividing tasks among group members does not remove the responsibility to understand the complete project.

## 4. Final Demonstration and Oral Examination

A final demonstration and oral examination will be conducted to assess the group's understanding of the submitted work.

During this evaluation, any group member may be asked questions about any part of the application, including code, architecture, functionality, and design decisions.

The evaluation will consider the group's collective understanding and ability to explain the submitted work.

If a group member cannot adequately explain the project, this may affect the evaluation of the entire group. Strong performance by other members will not automatically compensate for a lack of understanding within the group.

Therefore, please ensure that all group members are actively involved and reach a comparable level of understanding before the final evaluation.

## 5. Final Reminder

My expectations are straightforward:

Work together, document your individual contributions, understand the complete project, and submit only your group's own work.

These requirements will be enforced throughout the course.
